Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about filing or tracking your complaints? Find your answers below.

How do I track my complaint after submission? ▾
After submitting a complaint, a unique Complaint ID (e.g., CMP-2026-00101) is automatically generated. You can enter this ID on the "Track Complaint" page or view it directly in your "My Complaints" dashboard.
How long does it take for a complaint to be reviewed? ▾
Standard complaints are reviewed by the administration within 24 to 48 hours. Urgent priorities (such as safety or exam disputes) are evaluated immediately on the same business day.
Can I edit my contact details or department? ▾
Yes! Simply log in to your student account, go to the "Profile" page, update your phone number, email, or department, and click save.
Can I submit complaints for different campus amenities? ▾
Yes. The portal supports 12 distinct categories including Academic, Hostel, Canteen, IT, Transportation, Library, Infrastructure, and Examination.
